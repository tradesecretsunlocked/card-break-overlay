How the buyer name and artwork work

BUYER NAME: click anything on the board, a scrolling card, a spot tile or an envelope, and type the buyer in. Enter saves, Escape cancels. Click a sold one again to correct the name or unsell it. Sales arriving from the bridge fill the name in on their own.

ARTWORK: an upload stays in THIS browser only. For art that works on any machine, commit the file to images/pokemon/ named after the card, so "Charizard EX" becomes charizard-ex.png, and leave the upload empty.
