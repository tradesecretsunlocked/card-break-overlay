How automation reads a sale: the Whatnot title is checked for the delayed reveal name with a number (Delayed Reveal 7, DR 7) first, then for an item name or one of its "also matches" words. The longest match wins. If two items match equally, or nothing matches, the sale waits in the sold list (amber) for you to assign it. A delayed reveal title with no number takes the next open tile.
